# In-app Notifications on comparable learning platforms

**Summary.** Across Teachable, Thinkific, Udemy, Coursera and Canvas, the documented default channel for *course* events is **email, not an in-app feed**. Where an in-app bell exists (Udemy for instructors, Thinkific and Teachable only inside their Community add-ons), it covers a narrow set of conversational events: Q&A/comments, replies, mentions, and comments on announcements. Instructor-facing system events (enrollments, sales, reviews, completions) mostly arrive as per-kind emails or **daily digests** (Udemy, Teachable, Thinkific). Instructor Announcements go to **currently enrolled Students** by email and are also **kept on the course page** (Udemy, Canvas, Coursera). Canvas is the only one that documents edit-after-send: the Instructor chooses whether an edit re-notifies. Read state is a per-user flag. The docs only describe "open/click marks read" plus a mark-all action (Thinkific community, GitHub). Retention is documented only by GitHub (3 months unless Saved). GitHub models a Notification as one row per **thread** with a `reason`, an `unread` flag and `last_read_at`, and its clients **poll** (`Last-Modified`/`X-Poll-Interval`) instead of using a live push. That matches Cadence's plan to deliver on page load. None of the course platforms documents whether its bell is live or loads on page refresh.

> Method note: support.udemy.com, business-support.udemy.com and support.thinkific.com returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher. The Instructure Community guides and coursera.support are JavaScript-rendered and fetched as empty pages. For those vendors, findings marked **(excerpt)** come from the search engine's excerpt of the cited official page, not a full read. Teachable, GitHub and the Canvas REST API docs were read in full.

## Teachable

- **Events → roles.** Admin emails are toggled per user in Settings > Notifications. The **enrollment** group (new Student signs up, paid enrollment, free enrollment) goes to primary owners, owners, authors and affiliates. The **comment** group goes to owners and authors: new comment needing moderation, new comment in your course, new comment in a thread you've commented on, and a reply to your comment. The **subscription** group (payment failed, subscription cancelled) goes to owners only ([Email Notifications](https://support.teachable.com/en/articles/11682445-email-notifications)).
- **Student notifications.** Owners can switch each automated Student email on or off: certificates, comments in a lecture discussion the Student is part of, drip content, automatic unenrollment, free-product receipt, and lead double opt-in ([Email Notifications](https://support.teachable.com/en/articles/11682445-email-notifications)). Students can opt out of comment notifications in their own profile ([Course Comments](https://support.teachable.com/en/articles/11682464-course-comments)).
- **Click behaviour.** The admin comment email contains "a link to respond directly to the comment in your school's admin" ([Course Comments](https://support.teachable.com/en/articles/11682464-course-comments)).
- **In-app.** No in-app bell for courses is documented. Digests, frequency settings and read state are not documented ([Email Notifications](https://support.teachable.com/en/articles/11682445-email-notifications)). The Community, now "in maintenance mode", has per-category bell levels including **Muted**, a "Live notifications" toggle, and a Notifications list under Profile > Summary ([Create and manage community](https://support.teachable.com/en/articles/11682449-create-and-manage-community)).
- **Announcements.** There is no Announcement feature. The Emails tab sends emails to users and keeps a "history of emails sent from your school" ([Emails](https://support.teachable.com/en/articles/11682401-emails)). Edit after send and visibility to later enrollees are not documented.

## Thinkific

- **Events → roles.** Admin notification emails cover new sign-up, new order, new payout, discussion posts and replies, and reviews. Site Admins get Discussions/Assignments/Communities emails. Course Admins get Discussions and Assignments only for the courses assigned to them (excerpt: [Automated Email Notifications](https://support.thinkific.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039095614-Automated-Email-Notifications), [Course Admins](https://support.thinkific.com/hc/en-us/articles/360030735613-Course-Admins)). A course-completion email (at 100%) can be routed to a chosen site admin ([Can I get notified when my student completes a course?](https://support.thinkific.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048909674-Can-I-get-notified-when-my-student-completes-a-course)). A Free Preview enrollment produces no "New order" email (excerpt: [Free Preview notifications](https://support.thinkific.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060749714-Will-I-receive-notifications-when-a-student-enrolls-in-a-Free-Preview)).
- **In-app bell (Communities only).** The bell shows only if the site has at least one community, and the notification centre "is not accessible within the Course Player". The badge counts unopened notifications. A notification stays unread until opened. The list has All/Unread tabs and a mark-all-as-read action. Triggers are: added to a community, an admin's new post, mentions, comments on your or followed posts, and replies. Students can turn off email globally, but "it is not possible to turn off web app, in-app, or push notifications or customize which notifications you want to receive" (excerpt: [Community notifications](https://support.thinkific.com/hc/en-us/articles/7300696848407-Communities-Web-Mobile-and-Push-Notifications)).
- **Noise control.** The Weekly Reminder Email goes out once a week (Sunday, UTC) to enrolled Students (excerpt: [Weekly Reminder Emails](https://support.thinkific.com/hc/en-us/articles/360030717833-Weekly-Reminder-Emails)).
- **Announcements.** There is no Announcement feature. Students get **no automatic notification** when new content is added. Thinkific recommends a mass email or an external email tool instead (excerpt: [How do I notify students about course updates?](https://support.thinkific.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051972413-How-do-I-notify-students-about-course-updates)). Mass User Email can filter by enrollment status, but "mass emails are not saved within your dashboard" (excerpt: [Mass User Email](https://support.thinkific.com/hc/en-us/articles/360030717933-Mass-User-Email)).
- **Live vs page load.** Not documented.

## Udemy

- **Events → roles (Instructor).** The bell shows new Q&A questions and responses, comments on course announcements, assignment feedback, and caption confirmations, with a red number for unread items. Email digests cover the rest: a Daily Q&A Digest and a New Student & Review Digest. Transactional emails, Q&A reply notifications and direct messages cannot be disabled. Per-course settings control lecture-ready emails and the Q&A digest (excerpt: [Instructor Account Notifications](https://support.udemy.com/hc/articles/115008393928-Instructor-Account-Notifications)).
- **Students.** Account-level Notification Preferences exist. A Student can turn off instructor announcement emails **for one course**. Following a Q&A question notifies the follower on new responses (excerpt: [Manage notification preferences](https://business-support.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/12814600533271-How-to-manage-your-Udemy-notification-and-communication-preferences), [Q&A rules](https://support.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/229605848-Q-A-Rules-and-guidelines)). No Student-side bell is documented.
- **Announcements.** Instructors compose them from Communication > Announcements, with drafts and preview. Only an instructor who is visible on the course and has the manage permission can send. Educational announcements are limited to "four ... per course per month to students enrolled in the course". They are "delivered ... to the email inboxes of learners, and are also posted in the 'Announcement' section of your course". Students can comment, and those comments notify the Instructor (excerpt: [Send Promotional Emails and Educational Announcements](https://support.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/229605788-Send-Promotional-Emails-and-Educational-Announcements), [Announcements Rules](https://support.udemy.com/hc/articles/229605828-Announcements-Rules-and-Guidelines)). Analytics report unique views and unsubscribe rate ([Announcement analytics](https://support.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004428847-Educational-Announcement-and-Promotional-Email-Analytics)). Edit or delete after send is **not documented**. *Inference:* because announcements are posted on the course, later enrollees probably see past ones, but no doc says so.

## Coursera

- **Preferences.** Settings > Communication Preferences offers category checkboxes and an "Opt-out of all Coursera emails" box. "Communication about courses you are enrolled in" covers announcements, reminders and forum notifications (excerpt: [Check and update your email communication preferences](https://www.coursera.support/s/article/learner-000001611?language=en_US)).
- **Threads.** Following a forum post sends email updates. Course-level preferences can turn off emails from a course (excerpt: [Change email settings for the forums](https://www.coursera.support/s/article/learner-000001272?language=en_US)).
- **Announcements.** Instructors use course "announcements" to share information ([Third-party tools](https://www.coursera.support/s/article/learner-000001516?language=en_US), excerpt). The instructor-side tool, audience rules, edit after send, the in-app bell, read state and live delivery are all **not documented** in the public Learner Help Center.

## Canvas LMS

- **Model.** Preferences are per user and do not control other users' notifications. Account-level settings apply to all courses, and a course-level setting overrides them. Each notification type × contact method can be set to notify immediately, daily summary, weekly summary or off. "Notify immediately" may be "delayed up to 1 hour" to avoid floods when Instructors make several changes. Push notifications are immediate or off (excerpt: [Manage notification settings (Student Guide)](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Student-Guide/How-do-I-manage-my-Canvas-notification-settings-as-a-student/ta-p/434), [Manage notifications for a single course](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Basics-Guide/How-do-I-manage-notifications-for-a-single-course/ta-p/615325)). The API confirms the frequency values `immediately`, `daily`, `weekly`, `never`. A preference is stored per channel × notification (e.g. `new_announcement`), and bulk updates can work by category ([Notification Preferences API](https://canvas.instructure.com/doc/api/notification_preferences.html)).
- **In-app feed.** The activity stream mixes DiscussionTopic, Announcement, Conversation, Submission and other item types. Each item has `read_state`. A summary endpoint returns `count` and `unread_count` per type. Users can hide one item or all items ("Hide all stream items"). Retention and how `read_state` changes are not documented ([Users API – activity stream](https://canvas.instructure.com/doc/api/users.html)).
- **Announcements.** Announcements are stored as discussion topics, listed per course by date range. "Announcements scheduled for future posting will only be returned to course administrators" ([Announcements API](https://canvas.instructure.com/doc/api/announcements.html)). Students get announcement notifications only if the course is published. When editing, an Instructor can "save ... without notifying users or send a notification" (excerpt: [Edit an announcement](https://community.instructure.com/en/kb/articles/660645-how-do-i-edit-an-announcement-in-a-course)). An announcement whose "Available from" date is in the future sends no notification until it becomes available (excerpt, quoted from the guide in a community thread: [Prompt asking to notify students…](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Question-Forum/Prompt-asking-to-notify-students-of-a-change-when-there-is-a/m-p/616933)). Imported or copied announcements do not send new-announcement notifications (excerpt: [Copy an announcement](https://community.instructure.com/en/kb/articles/660653-how-do-i-copy-an-announcement-to-another-course)). Whether later enrollees see past announcements is **not documented**. *Inference:* the course Announcements page lists all posted announcements to anyone who can view the course, so they probably do.
- **Live vs page load.** Not documented.

## GitHub (general reference)

- **Model.** A notification is a **thread** (issue, PR or commit discussion) with a `reason` such as mention, review requested, assigned or state change. The reason can change over time. Each thread has a boolean `unread` and a `last_read_at` ([REST: notifications](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/activity/notifications)).
- **Subscriptions.** Users are subscribed automatically when they participate, are assigned or are mentioned. They can watch a repository with a custom per-event filter, ignore a repository, or unsubscribe from a thread, which mutes it "until you are @mentioned" ([About notifications](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/managing-subscriptions-and-notifications-on-github/setting-up-notifications/about-notifications), [Configuring notifications](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/managing-subscriptions-and-notifications-on-github/setting-up-notifications/configuring-notifications)).
- **Read state.** The inbox has read/unread, **Done** (removes the item from the inbox), Saved, and bulk triage. Mark-all-as-read accepts `last_read_at`, so items updated after that time stay unread, and a large batch can complete asynchronously with a 202 ([Managing notifications from your inbox](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/managing-subscriptions-and-notifications-on-github/viewing-and-triaging-notifications/managing-notifications-from-your-inbox), [REST: notifications](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/activity/notifications)).
- **Retention.** "Notifications that are not marked as Saved are kept for 3 months". Saved items are kept indefinitely ([About notifications](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/managing-subscriptions-and-notifications-on-github/setting-up-notifications/about-notifications)).
- **Delivery.** The API is "optimized for polling with the `Last-Modified` header" (a 304 when nothing is new), and `X-Poll-Interval` throttles clients ([REST: notifications](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/activity/notifications)). The docs do not say whether the web inbox itself updates live.

## Cross-platform patterns

1. **Email does the heavy lifting.** In-app bells, where they exist, are for conversation events: Q&A, comments, replies, mentions. No course platform documents an in-app bell for Students covering course events. Cadence's in-app-only plan is therefore **unusual**: the bell has to carry what peers send by email.
2. **System events go to the business side, often as digests.** Enrollments, sales, reviews, subscription failures and completions go to owners and Instructors (Teachable, Thinkific, Udemy), often batched daily (Udemy). Thinkific scopes course events to the admins assigned to the course.
3. **Thread scoping limits noise.** Comment notifications go to people in that thread or followers (Teachable, Udemy Q&A, Coursera forums, GitHub). GitHub stores one row per thread.
4. **Preferences are per kind, and often per course.** Canvas and Udemy allow per-course overrides. Several platforms make some kinds mandatory (Udemy transactional and Q&A replies, Thinkific community in-app).
5. **Announcements are email plus a page on the course.** Only enrolled Students receive the email (Udemy). The course page keeps the history. Canvas lets the author choose whether an edit re-notifies, and scheduled or imported posts do not notify.
6. **Read state is per user.** It is marked on open, with mark-all. Only GitHub documents retention (3 months). Only GitHub documents its delivery mechanism, and that mechanism is polling.

## Implications for the open tickets

**#4 Which events notify whom.** Peers send system events to the business side: Enrollment and Refund/subscription events go to the owner or Instructor, scoped to the Instructor's own courses (Thinkific Course Admins, Teachable "authors"). Comments and lesson questions should notify the Instructor of the course plus thread participants. Students should be notified about replies to them, not about every comment (Teachable, Udemy, GitHub). Peers batch Enrollments and reviews into digests (Udemy). With no email, Cadence could collapse repeated events, for example "5 new Enrollments in X", instead of creating one Notification each (*inference*). Quiz attempts are not documented as notifications anywhere except Canvas's per-type preferences. Course ratings go to Instructors (Thinkific "New review", Udemy digest and mobile push). No peer documents notifications for Team Licence Seats or for Team Admins, so that is an open design space. The Announcement Notification goes to currently enrolled Students only (matches Udemy).

**#5 How Announcements work.** Writer: the course's Instructor. Udemy requires the instructor to be visible on the course with manage permission, which suggests asking whether a Platform Admin can also post. Audience: currently enrolled Students, matching Udemy. Edit after send: Canvas offers "save silently" or "notify again", so Cadence should decide whether an edit updates the delivered Notification in place (computed from the live Announcement) or creates a new one. Delete after send is not documented by any peer. Later enrollees: peers keep Announcements on a course page that every current Student can see. Cadence should keep that history on the course page but not back-fill Notifications to later enrollees (*inference*; Canvas does not notify for imported or future-dated posts). Consider a rate limit (Udemy: 4 per month).

**#6 The bell and list.** Use a bell with an unread count badge (Udemy red number, Thinkific badge). Use a dropdown, with All/Unread tabs and mark-all-as-read either there or on a full page (Thinkific). Mark a Notification read when it is opened or clicked (Thinkific "unread until opened"). Clicking a Notification deep-links to the thing itself (Teachable's link to the comment). Hiding or Done is optional (Canvas hide, GitHub Done). Make mark-all bounded by the time the list was loaded, like GitHub's `last_read_at`, so Notifications that arrive later stay unread. Page-load delivery is consistent with GitHub's polling model, and no peer documents a live bell.

**#7 Stored or computed.** GitHub and Canvas expose stored per-user rows with read state (`unread`/`last_read_at`, `read_state`). That points to stored rows for per-user events. For an Announcement to a large course, a hybrid avoids fanning out at write time (*inference*): derive "Announcements in my enrolled courses since my Enrollment" at read time, and store only per-user read state. That also makes edits and deletes show up automatically, and lets the Announcement audience follow the Students who are enrolled now. Retention: GitHub keeps 3 months. Cadence needs its own rule. Preference storage should be keyed by user × kind (× course), with a channel column so email and push can be added later. This matches the Canvas preference shape.

### Surprising findings / new open questions

- Thinkific Students **cannot** turn off in-app notifications at all. Should Cadence offer per-kind or per-course mute for Students in v1?
- Canvas holds back "immediate" notifications for up to an hour to batch an Instructor's rapid edits. Should Cadence collapse repeated events in a time window?
- Canvas sends no Notification for scheduled or imported Announcements and lets the author choose whether an edit re-notifies. Does Cadence need scheduled Announcements and a "notify again" choice?
- Udemy caps educational announcements at 4 per course per month. Should Cadence rate-limit Announcements?
- No peer documents in-app notifications for Team or seat events. Who is told when a Team Licence Seat is assigned or revoked: the Student, the Team Admin, or both?
- Some kinds are mandatory on peers (Udemy Q&A replies and transactional emails). Should Refund and Enrollment Notifications be exempt from muting?
